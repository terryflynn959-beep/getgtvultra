Amazon Fire TV Stick setup guide

Use this guide to prepare your Fire TV Stick and troubleshoot basic device issues. App availability and installation methods can vary by model, region and app provider.

Safety first: Use only apps and content you are authorised to access. Follow your device manufacturer's guidance and the terms of your internet, app and content providers. Never bypass security warnings.

1. Connect your Fire TV Stick

Plug the Fire TV Stick into an available HDMI port and connect its power adapter. Select the matching HDMI input on your television.

2. Connect to your home Wi-Fi

Follow the on-screen setup and choose your home Wi-Fi network. Enter your Wi-Fi password on the device itself; never send it to support.

3. Check for device updates

Open Settings, then My Fire TV or Device & Software, and check for available system updates. Menu names can differ by model.

4. Find a compatible player app

Use the Amazon Appstore or the app provider's verified instructions. Check that the app is compatible with your Fire TV model and region.

5. If an app will not open

Restart the Fire TV Stick, check available storage, install official updates and reopen the app. If the problem remains, note the app version and device model.

6. If the picture or sound is missing

Check the selected HDMI input, reseat the device, restart the TV and Fire TV Stick, and test another HDMI port if available.

Frequently asked questions

Can every Fire TV Stick use every app?

No. Compatibility depends on the device generation, software version, region and app provider. Check the official app listing.

Should I disable device security to install an app?

No. Do not disable security protections or install files from unknown sources. Use official stores or a download source verified by the app provider.

What should I send support?

Send the Fire TV model, app name and version, the exact error message and a screenshot with personal information hidden. Never send passwords.
