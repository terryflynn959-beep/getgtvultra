About GTV ULTRA

GTV ULTRA is a UK customer hub that brings app information, simple setup guides and practical help together in one easy-to-use place, so you can spend less time troubleshooting and more time enjoying your entertainment.

Who we are

We are a customer-focused support team helping people get set up and stay connected on the devices they already use, including Amazon Fire TV Stick, Android TV, Google TV and Smart TVs. Our aim is to make setup straightforward, explain problems in plain English and give every customer a friendly person to talk to when they need help.

What you will find here

App information

Verified app download information, portal details and device compatibility notes in one place.

Setup guides

Step-by-step, picture-led guides and video tutorials for popular streaming devices and Smart TVs.

Troubleshooting help

Practical fixes for buffering, login errors, no-signal messages and app installation problems.

Reseller information

A dedicated help desk and guidelines for resellers working with the GTV ULTRA team.

What we stand for

Clear, honest help

Simple instructions without jargon, and straightforward answers about what is and is not supported.

Safety first

We will never ask for your password, one-time codes or payment card details in a chat. We encourage safe, official app sources only.

Responsible use

Use only apps, accounts and content you are legally authorised to access, and follow your provider's terms.

Real support

If a guide does not solve your problem, our team is available on WhatsApp to help you directly.
